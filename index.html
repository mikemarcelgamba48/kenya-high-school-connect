Privacy and safety

This prototype stores profile details, posts and notes in local browser storage. It does not create a real account or upload information. Avoid sharing personal contact details, passwords, home addresses, private images or precise location.

Real learner accounts, cross-device posts, private messaging and moderation require a secure backend, appropriate school/guardian safeguards, and trusted human moderators. These are not active in this version.
